The fine print, in plain words

Points will be considered for mainnet genesis allocation. No amounts, conversion rate or allocation formula have been set, and none is promised here. tQPF itself has no monetary value.

Points reward real participation. Activity that looks like farming (many wallets or validators run by one person, self-transfers, scripted loops) may be excluded or adjusted before any allocation. Rules can change during the season; changes will be posted here.

Separately, $QPF holders migrate 1:1 to native QPF at mainnet via snapshot, as stated on the roadmap.
