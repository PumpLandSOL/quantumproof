// the threat

Every chain you hold today signs with math a quantum computer breaks.

Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana all rely on elliptic-curve signatures. Shor's algorithm solves the elliptic-curve discrete log in polynomial time. Once your public key is on-chain, a large enough quantum machine can derive your private key from it.

// how it works

Three layers, zero elliptic curves.

01

Lattice keys in your browser

Your wallet generates an ML-DSA-44 keypair locally. The secret key never leaves your device. Your address is a SHA3-256 hash of the public key.

addr = "qpf1" ‖ SHA3-256(pk)[0..20]
02

Verified at the gate

Every transaction carries its 1,312-byte public key and 2,420-byte signature. The node verifies it against FIPS 204 before it touches the mempool.

verify(ML-DSA-44, pk, canon(tx), σ)
03

Quantum-safe consensus

Proposers rotate round-robin. A block is final when the proposer plus at least two attesters sign its SHA3-256 header hash with ML-DSA-65.

final ⇔ 1 + ≥2 valid ML-DSA-65 sigs
