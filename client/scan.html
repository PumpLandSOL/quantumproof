01Your public key leaks

Solana addresses are Ed25519 public keys. Bitcoin and Ethereum reveal the public key the first time a wallet signs a spend. Taproot shows it from day one.

02Q-day reverses it

A large fault-tolerant quantum computer running Shor's algorithm derives the private key from the public key. Every exposed wallet becomes spendable by someone else.

03Lattices don't leak

QuantumProof signs with ML-DSA (NIST FIPS 204). There's no known quantum attack on it, and no elliptic-curve key ever touches the chain.
