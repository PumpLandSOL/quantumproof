How it works
  1. One transaction, two instructions. A Token-2022 TransferChecked of $QPF to the migration wallet AdjV…Xsgt ↗, and a memo qpf-migrate:v1:<qpf1>.
  2. Only you can do it. Your Solana key signs both, so nobody can move your tokens or redirect them to another address.
  3. Checked on Solana. The server fetches the confirmed transaction and checks the signer, the mint, the recipient, the amount and the memo.
  4. 1 : 1 at genesis. Every allocation is public in the genesis file, each backed by its Solana signature.
