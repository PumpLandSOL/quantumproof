Every Solana address is an Ed25519 public key, the exact thing Shor's algorithm reverses. The Lifeboat binds your Solana wallet to a post-quantum QuantumProof address with two signatures over one message. Your real $QPF balance sets your tier.

01

Connect your Solana wallet

We read your address and your live $QPF balance on Solana mainnet. Nothing is moved, nothing is approved.

02

Your post-quantum key

An ML-DSA-44 keypair (NIST FIPS 204), generated in this browser. If you already have a QuantumProof wallet here, we use it.

03

Sign twice, board once

Phantom signs the boarding message with Ed25519. Your browser signs the same message with ML-DSA-44. The server verifies both and writes the binding into the next block.
