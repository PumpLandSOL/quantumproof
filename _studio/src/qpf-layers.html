// how it works
Three layers. Zero elliptic curves.
01
Keys in your browser
ML-DSA-44 keypair generated on your device. The secret never leaves it.
addr = "qpf1" ‖ SHA3-256(pk)
02
Verified at the gate
Every tx carries a 1,312 B key and 2,420 B signature, checked against FIPS 204 before the mempool.
verify(ML-DSA-44, pk, tx, σ)
03
Quantum-safe consensus
Each block is signed by its proposer and attested by the other validators with ML-DSA-65.
final ⇔ 1 + ≥2 valid sigs
